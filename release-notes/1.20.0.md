## 1.20.0 — Airport Focus, Flight Setup & News

- Reworks the Scratchpad into a compact EFB-style writing surface with a bright paper canvas, clearer tool groups and a better ultrawide/tablet layout.
- Renames **Sim Session** to **Flight Setup** and removes the duplicate Flight Deck quick-launch grid. Flight Setup now focuses on the actual simulator environment: MSFS, ATC/network tools, support applications, screenshots and tablet connectivity.
- Extends Flight Setup detection beyond Windows EXE/process scanning. Flight Deck now also scans the MSFS 2024 Community and Official/Marketplace package locations and marks add-ons that are installed inside the simulator even when they do not have their own executable.
- Makes **Settings** the final app in the Flight Deck home launcher regardless of saved app order or pilot profile.
- Adds an installable **News** app with a curated catalog of 20 flight-simulation sources including FSElite, cruiselevel, MSFS Addons, Threshold, Flightsim.to News and FSNews. Feeds can be installed or removed individually and articles always open at the original publisher.
- Adds low-frequency news refresh and optional native Windows notifications for newly published articles while Flight Deck is running.
- Changes the Taxi/Airport map to a strict airport-only presentation: surroundings outside the aerodrome are fully masked, map panning is clamped to the airport area and the OSM background is reduced to a subtle reference layer beneath Flight Deck's airport vectors.
- Hardens the News and Flight Setup UI against recursive DOM-observer refresh loops and keeps the 1.19 migration compatible with the expanded 1.20 host services.

> Flight simulation use only — not for real-world navigation.
