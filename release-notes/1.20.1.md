## 1.20.1 — Unified UI & In-App News

- Fixes unreadable dark-on-dark combinations in Scratchpad, Flight Setup and News by making all three modules explicitly theme-aware in both Light and Dark mode.
- Aligns the new modules with Flight Deck's existing visual system: the same panel surfaces, borders, typography hierarchy, spacing, buttons, status accents and card geometry used throughout the established apps.
- Keeps the Scratchpad writing surface bright and high-contrast while the surrounding controls follow the selected Flight Deck theme.
- Reworks Flight Setup cards and tool rows to match the established EFB modules instead of using a separate visual language.
- Reworks News cards, feed management and filters to use the same Flight Deck design language.
- Adds a native **in-app News reader**. Selecting an article now opens it inside Flight Deck rather than immediately leaving the app.
- News feeds can provide up to 12,000 characters of feed-supplied article text to the in-app reader. If a publisher only exposes a teaser in RSS/Atom, Flight Deck clearly keeps an **Open Original** action for the complete article.
- Unread News are marked as read when the article is actually opened instead of marking the entire feed read merely by entering the News app.

> Flight simulation use only — not for real-world navigation.
