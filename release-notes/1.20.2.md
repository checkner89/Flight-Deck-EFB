## 1.20.2 — Unified Apps, Scratchpad Colors & Rich News

- Makes **Scratchpad, Flight Setup and News** use the same Home tile hierarchy, geometry, typography and strong icon treatment as the established Flight Deck apps.
- Fixes the Scratchpad writing surface so the actual canvas is explicitly rendered as bright paper in both Light and Dark mode instead of inheriting a black canvas surface.
- Adds independent **pen and marker colors** with presets, custom color pickers and persistent per-device settings. Existing strokes remain compatible.
- Adds **image support to Scratchpad**: insert from file, drag & drop or clipboard paste; select, move, resize and remove images; PNG export includes images plus drawings.
- Keeps erasing scoped to the drawing layer so an eraser stroke does not destroy the inserted image layer.
- Reworks the News overview into denser, modern Flight Deck cards with publisher favicons/logos, optional article thumbnails, better source metadata, unread filtering and consistent controls.
- Adds an enhanced **in-app News reader** that requests the publicly available article on demand, extracts the main article region and preserves headings, paragraphs, lists, quotes and inline images when available.
- The enhanced reader does **not** bypass paywalls, logins or publisher restrictions. If full extraction is unavailable, Flight Deck falls back to the RSS/Atom content and keeps the Original action.
- Improves News feed management with publisher logos and clearer notification controls.
- Retains **Settings as the final Home app** and does not add duplicate functional modules.

> Flight simulation use only — not for real-world navigation.
